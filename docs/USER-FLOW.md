# VAILYF AI — User Flow

## Business journey
Meta Ads → Landing Page → ₹199 Webinar Registration → Zoom Webinar → ₹500 One-to-One Consultation Booking → ₹24,000 Premium Program Offer.

These are business stages, not guaranteed purchases. The ₹500 adjustment policy is pending confirmation; do not imply credit toward the premium program. The nine website screens cover discovery, assessment, registration and success. Downstream booking and program sales channels await approval.

## Primary registration flow
1. Visitor follows a Meta Ad to the Homepage or an approved campaign landing entry. Preserve approved campaign attribution without exposing personal data.
2. Homepage introduces the proposition and ₹199 registration path. The visitor can explore mentor, framework, assessment, learning outcomes, benefits, testimonials and FAQ in any order.
3. Any Register CTA opens Pricing & Registration, where the visitor reviews confirmed inclusions, schedule, duration and policies.
4. Visitor enters required, approved registration details. Invalid or incomplete fields receive clear inline feedback without losing valid entries.
5. Visitor selects Pay ₹199 and is handed to the chosen payment provider. Provider and payment methods are pending selection.
6. Trusted backend/service verification confirms payment and creates or retrieves a single registration.
7. Thank You / Success displays verified confirmation and approved Zoom access instructions. Access delivery and confirmation channels must be approved before implementation.
8. Visitor attends the Zoom webinar at the confirmed time.
9. After the webinar, an approved invitation leads interested attendees to a ₹500 one-to-one consultation booking destination. Scheduling, fee collection, cancellation and adjustment rules remain pending.
10. During or after consultation, the business may offer the ₹24,000 premium program through an approved sales process. No program outcome or conversion is guaranteed.

## Assessment branch
Circle of Life → assessment introduction → approved questions → completeness validation → approved scoring → indicative results → optional webinar registration.

Questions, dimensions, scale, scoring and results copy are pending. Do not invent a clinical interpretation. Proposed behavior retains answers only for the current session unless a different data policy is approved. Any email gate, saved result, partial progress recovery or CRM submission requires a separate decision.

## Alternate and recovery flows
- Visitor declines registration: retain access to discovery content without forcing checkout.
- Form errors: explain missing/invalid details and keep entered values.
- Checkout cancelled: return to registration with an option to retry; do not show success.
- Payment failed: show a clear failure state and retry/support options using approved destinations.
- Payment pending or callback delayed: show pending verification, then recheck trusted status; do not encourage a second payment while the first is unresolved.
- Browser refresh or duplicate callback: retrieve the same order/registration idempotently.
- Direct visit to `/thank-you` without verified status: show unverified/pending guidance and a registration/support path, with no false receipt or protected joining link.
- WebGL unavailable or reduced motion enabled: show readable static framework content and usable CTAs.
- Schedule changes, missed webinar and recording requests: use approved support and policy guidance; recordings are not promised.

## Consent and communications
Collect only approved necessary registration data and explain its use. Marketing consent, analytics/pixel behavior, WhatsApp tips and Telegram invitations require approved wording and applicable consent handling. Do not auto-enroll visitors into channels based only on their assessment participation.
